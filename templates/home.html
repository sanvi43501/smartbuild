About SmartBuild

SmartBuild is an integrated building lifecycle management system designed to bring different stages of building management together in one platform. The system focuses on Planning, Monitoring and Maintenance, including AI-based crack detection.

SmartBuild Modules

🏗️

Planning

Provides planning-related information to support the building lifecycle.

📊

Monitoring

Helps monitor construction activities, progress and project milestones.

🤖

Maintenance

Uses AI-based crack detection to identify cracks from building images.
